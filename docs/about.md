# 关于 AI Engineering Radar / About

## 中文

AI Engineering Radar 是一个开放的工程文章索引。首批仅追踪国内公司官方技术团队，帮助读者发现团队实践，回到原文阅读。本批暂不接入海外来源。

每篇文章保留公司、发布主体、原文地址和来源关系。仓库中的[来源验收表](source-acceptance.md)和[公开来源 JSON](https://liuminxin45.github.io/ai-engineering-radar/data/sources.json)记录官方身份的公开证据、核验状态和最近采集结果。官方团队可以发布在自有网站，也可以使用第三方博客社区；托管平台的域名不等于文章的公司归属。

项目从允许采集的入口提取必要元数据和原站提供的短介绍。已核验且启用的官方来源文章直接建立索引，保留身份核验、去重与数据校验，不做内容相关性筛选、模型审核或自动主题分类。

网站提供搜索和公司分列浏览。点击文章标题直接打开发布网站，每列触底时自动追加文章，右上角显示数据更新时间。你也可以使用全站 RSS / Atom 订阅，持续追踪整个项目的更新。文章保留原始语言，全文在原站阅读。项目不复制、存储或分发文章全文；代码的 MIT 许可不改变原文、摘要、名称和商标的权利归属。

顶部“博文 / 论文”切换共用公司分列看板。博文每家公司保留一个发布渠道，论文渠道按作者机构或公司自有研究目录另行核验。正式出版物使用出版方提交的机构记录，预印本核对论文作者区；联合论文可归属多家公司。详情页和公开 JSON 保留逐公司证据，归属反映论文发表时的机构信息。当前覆盖及未启用渠道见[论文来源核验](paper-sources.md)。

欢迎[提交 PR 适配国内官方新来源](../CONTRIBUTING.md)，包括第三方博客社区中的国内公司官方账号。请提供公司、发布主体、稳定账号 ID、官方证据、允许采集的入口，以及适配器 fixture 和测试。线索尚不完整时，也可以先提交[来源提议](https://github.com/liuminxin45/ai-engineering-radar/issues/new?template=source.yml)。登记、身份核验和采集成功分别记录，具体覆盖情况以[来源验收表](source-acceptance.md)为准。

发现错误归属、失效链接或版权问题时，请在[仓库 Issue](https://github.com/liuminxin45/ai-engineering-radar/issues)中提供文章地址和更正依据。

## English

AI Engineering Radar is an open index of engineering articles. This initial release follows official engineering teams at Chinese companies, helping readers discover their work and read it at the source. Overseas companies are outside this batch.

Each article keeps its company, publisher, original URL, and source relationships. The [source acceptance report](source-acceptance.md) and [public source JSON](https://liuminxin45.github.io/ai-engineering-radar/data/sources.json) record ownership evidence, verification status, and collection results. Teams may publish on their own sites or through third-party blogging communities. A hosting platform's domain does not establish who wrote an article.

The project collects essential metadata and short descriptions supplied by the original site from permitted endpoints. Articles from verified, enabled official sources enter the index directly, with identity verification, deduplication, and data validation. There is no content relevance screening, model review, or automatic topic classification.

Readers can search the index and browse company columns. Article titles open the publishing site directly, and each column loads another batch as readers reach its bottom. The update time appears at the top right. Subscribe to the site-wide RSS / Atom feeds to follow updates across the project. Articles keep their original language. Full articles remain on their original sites: this project does not copy, store, or redistribute their full text. The code's MIT license does not relicense source content, descriptions, names, or trademarks.

The Blog and Papers tabs share the company board. Paper affiliations come from publisher-deposited author institutions, paper author blocks, or official research directories. A joint paper may belong to multiple companies while keeping one index record. Per-company evidence links and short institution excerpts remain available in detail pages and public JSON. They describe institutions at publication time, not authors' current employment. See the [paper source audit](paper-sources.md) for coverage and unresolved channels.

We welcome [PRs that add or repair sources](../CONTRIBUTING.md) for Chinese companies, including official accounts on third-party blogging communities. Include the company, publisher, stable account ID, public ownership evidence, permitted collection endpoint, and adapter fixtures with tests. If you have an incomplete lead, [suggest a source](https://github.com/liuminxin45/ai-engineering-radar/issues/new?template=source.yml). Registration, verification, and successful collection are tracked separately; consult the [source acceptance report](source-acceptance.md) for current coverage.

For attribution errors, broken links, or content removal requests, open a [repository issue](https://github.com/liuminxin45/ai-engineering-radar/issues) with the article URL and supporting details.
